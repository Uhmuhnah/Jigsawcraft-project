Contact

For support, bug reports, policy requests, or copyright concerns, please contact us by email: muyucod@gmail.com. Include your browser, device, puzzle mode, and a screenshot when reporting a technical issue. This helps us reproduce and fix problems faster.

We usually respond within 3-5 business days. For legal and privacy requests, include a clear subject line such as "Privacy Request" or "Copyright Notice." If you are requesting removal of uploaded content, please provide the page URL and the reason for removal. We review all requests carefully and take action according to applicable law and platform policy.
