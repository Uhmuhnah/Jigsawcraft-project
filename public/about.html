About Jigsawcraft

Welcome to Jigsawcraft. This project is built for players who want a clean, high-quality online jigsaw experience without installation. You can upload your own photo, select puzzle difficulty, and start immediately in the browser. We focus on family-friendly play, responsive controls, and visual clarity so both quick sessions and long puzzle runs feel comfortable.

Great puzzle photos are important, but great puzzle interaction matters just as much. Jigsawcraft includes practical play features that help real puzzle solving: full-screen friendly layout, selectable piece counts (50/100/300/500/1000), optional piece rotation, border/color/region sorting, connected-group movement after snapping, and final PNG download when completed. We keep refining performance and usability for larger boards and more devices. If you have feedback or feature ideas, please contact us.

Game Guide

  1. Choose an image. Upload your image in Setup. For better puzzle quality, use a higher-resolution image, especially for 500 or 1000 pieces.
  2. Select difficulty. Pick 50, 100, 300, 500, or 1000 pieces. More pieces increase challenge and generation time.
  3. Set options. Turn on Ghost Image for visual guidance and Piece Rotation if you want extra difficulty with rotated pieces.
  4. Use Frame Tools. In 300+ modes, adjust the frame area and shape (Rectangle, Circle, Diamond, Heart) to choose the exact puzzle crop.
  5. Start and assemble. Drag pieces to connect neighboring parts. Connected pieces move as a group, and progress increases as more pieces join.
  6. Use Sort and Settings. In-game, use Sort to arrange pieces by color, border, or region. Use Settings to change floor theme for comfort.
  7. Complete inside the frame. Finish the puzzle in the board frame to clear the game. The timer tracks your result.
  8. Download result. After completion, use Download PNG to save the assembled puzzle image.
